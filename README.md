# TradeProtocol

### Trade with discipline. Manage risk. Build consistency.

TradeProtocol is a mobile trading journal designed to help traders develop better trading habits, maintain discipline, manage risk, and evaluate their trading performance.

It provides a structured environment for documenting trades, reviewing trading decisions, tracking emotions, and maintaining accountability throughout the trading process.

---

## Overview

Trading successfully requires more than identifying market opportunities. It requires consistency, patience, risk management, and emotional control.

TradeProtocol brings these elements together through a structured journaling and trade-review experience.

## Key Features

### 1. Trade Management
- Record and organise individual trades.
- Track trade direction: BUY or SELL.
- Record trading strategies and market timeframes.
- Categorise trades by trading session.
- Track wins, losses, breakeven, and open positions.

### 2. Trading Discipline Checklist
A structured checklist encourages traders to follow their trading rules.

- Pre-market preparation.
- Market trend and key-zone identification.
- Setup confirmation.
- Risk assessment.
- Stop-loss and take-profit planning.
- Emotional readiness.
- During-trade discipline.
- Post-trade evaluation.

### 3. Risk Management
- Position sizing and lot-size review.
- Risk assessment before entering trades.
- Stop-loss verification.
- Take-profit planning.
- Risk-to-reward evaluation, including a 1:2 target guideline.

### 4. Trading Psychology
- Evaluate emotional readiness before entering trades.
- Document emotions after trading.
- Recognise impulsive trading behaviour.
- Identify recurring mistakes.
- Reflect on trading decisions.

### 5. Trade Evidence
- Attach images to individual trades.
- Organise evidence into categories.
- Document trade setups and market analysis.
- Record mistakes and lessons learned.
- Review evidence from previous trades.

### 6. Strategies and Trading Sessions

**Supported strategies:**
- Supply and Demand
- Support and Resistance
- Trend Following
- Breakout
- Reversal
- Scalping
- ICT Order Flow
- Price Action
- Other

**Supported timeframes:**
M1, M5, M15, M30, H1, H4, D1, W1.

**Trading sessions:**
London, New York, Asian, Overlap, Other.

### 7. Trading Accountability
- Losing-streak awareness.
- Daily trade-count awareness.
- Pre-save intervention checks.
- Reinforcement of trading discipline.
